Inside Letathuske Dk

Letathuske Dk operates as a yoga studio based in San Diego, CA. We teach alignment you can feel—clear cues, respectful rooms, and a schedule that fits real weeks, not perfect ones. This site is the public version of how we work day to day—hours, offerings, and how to reach a person.
